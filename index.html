Candy Call privacy policy

Call a colour. Clear their line. Don't run out first.

Effective 22 November 2026.

Candy Call is a two-player candy line game you play on one phone, or against the phone. It has no accounts and no server of its own. This policy explains what the app does with information, in plain words.

What stays on your phone

  • Settings and names. Line size, match length, sound, vibration, the rules and phone level you last chose, and the player names you type for "Play with a friend" are saved on the phone so you don't have to enter them again.
  • Stats. Your wins and losses against the phone, and how many matches you've played with a friend, are saved on the phone.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. Clear it with Reset stats in the app, in Android Settings → Apps → Candy Call → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.

Candy Call never reads your contacts, messages, photos, files, location, microphone, camera, phone number or accounts.

Vibration on each popped candy uses the phone's standard touch feedback and needs no permission.

Sharing a result

A result image leaves your phone only when you tap Share result and choose an app (for example WhatsApp or Instagram). It then goes where you send it. The image shows the game name, the score, the rules and level, and the names typed for a friend match (if any). Nothing else.

Ads

Candy Call is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home and stats screens, a full-screen ad after a finished match (never during a match, and never after the first match each time you open the app), and an optional video ad if you choose to peek at the phone's front candy.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • app interactions with ads and diagnostics (such as crash information from the ads SDK),
  • basic device information (model, operating system, language).

Google's use of this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings under Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

Apart from what the ads SDK collects and crash reports (below), no information leaves your phone.

Crash reports

If Candy Call crashes, it sends a crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics so the problem can be fixed. A crash report contains technical details only: the error and where in the app it happened, the phone model, Android version, app version, free memory and storage at the time, and a random installation ID that isn't linked to you. It never contains player names, stats or your game. Google's terms for Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Children

Candy Call is made for teens and adults (13+): couples, friends and families playing together. It is not directed at children under 13.
